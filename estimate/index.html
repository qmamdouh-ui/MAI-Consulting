Price estimate

Build the engagement you need and see its price in delivery days.

Every delivery day is EUR 800, whatever the type of organisation. Choose the scope and the page shows the days, where each one comes from, and the total. Nothing here is a quote, since the scope is fixed in writing after a 30-minute call, but the number should be visible before you book that call.

1 · What kind of organisation are you?

The rate is the same for every type of organisation: EUR 800 per delivery day. Your answer tells us who we are speaking with. Individuals and organisation representatives take the course, which is priced per group and shared among the participants.

2 · The course

A course for fewer than ten participants, built on the participants' own tasks. Three delivery days reach level 3, which is 3 × EUR 800 = EUR 2,400 for the group, shared among the participants: one person pays EUR 2,400, four pay EUR 600 each, nine pay about EUR 267 each. Higher target levels add the days in the ladder. Online it costs tuition only. In person, the Geneva venue, hotel nights, lunches and breaks, and the arrangements around them are itemised below as estimates and invoiced at actual cost.

2 · How many staff would this cover?

Size drives how many departments we work with and how many sittings a session needs, so it moves the day count. The rate does not change.

3 · Readiness assessment

The assessment produces the baseline score, the inventory of tools in use and the ranked gap list the rest of the work is built on. Two depths, with different day counts.

Four essential dimensionsThe four competencies of AI fluency, scored across the organisation with a short interview round. It answers what staff can and cannot do, and what to train first. It does not cover governance, data protection, workforce impact or psychological safety.
Ten dimensionsThe full instrument: sixty questions, staff interviews and a documented tool inventory, with the optional standards mapping in the vocabulary of ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF and EU AI Act Article 4. Choose this when a board, a funder or an auditor will read the report, or when the question is governance rather than skills. It is a management report, not certification preparation.

4 · Training

Training is scoped by where the group starts and where it needs to reach. The seven levels, the days between them and what each one changes are set out on the training page.

What each level means
Level 1No working use of an assistant yet. Nothing to unlearn, and the objections are specific enough to answer with the team's own documents.
Level 2Occasional use with no shared method: a summary here, a search there. Output varies by person and by day because the input does.
Level 3Structured prompting and verification. The person briefs the work as they would a colleague, gives the source document, and checks figures, facts, language and defensibility before anything is sent. This is the floor we train to.
Level 4Persistent set-ups. Projects hold the reference documents, custom instructions hold the standing rules, skills hold the house method. Nothing is briefed twice.
Level 5Delegated work. An agent carries a job from brief to draft while the person supervises, and several workstreams run at once.
Level 6Building for others. Designs agents and skills for colleagues, connects the sources, and reviews the team's AI-assisted output. This is what makes the capability survive turnover.
Level 7Terminal-based agentic work with no coding background. Claude Code operates on folders of files rather than one conversation: batch document work, the same review across a year of reports, a repeatable pipeline.

Which departments need their own lab and playbook? Each lab works on that team's real tasks and ends in its playbook.Department labA working session with one department on its own tasks and documents. Department sizeLarger departments need more sittings to cover everyone and a wider set of documents to test against, so a lab costs more. Where departments differ in size, say so in the notes and we will scope each one properly.

General means one umbrella policy: permitted uses, red lines, data classification, disclosure, escalation, approved tools. Specific annexes add rules for a particular department or use (casework data, fundraising, HR records) where the umbrella is not enough.

6 · Custom agents, skills and tools

Three different things, counted separately: about 3 delivery days per agent, 1.5 per skill and 2 per tool, scaled by the size of the organisation and of the department that will use them, because testing and handover grow with both. Each is configured on the platform you already use, tested on your documents and handed over with an operating guide. 7 · Fluency Support

A monthly retainer after the engagement: office hours for whoever is stuck, playbook revisions as the tools change, coaching for your internal champions, and a re-score at six months. One delivery day a month is EUR 800 a month; two days are EUR 1,600. The six-month re-score adds 2 delivery days once. See what a support month contains →

8 · Which tasks are you trying to improve?

Each task you pick is built into the training material and the playbooks: the patterns, the worked examples and the checks are prepared for that task on your documents. The first three carry most of the preparation, and further ones cost less.

9 · Anything we should know

Languages, deadlines, sites, data constraints, a funder requirement, or a task not listed above. This is sent with your estimate.

How to read the number

How to read the number.

The number is delivery days × EUR 800. One rate, for every type of client: NGO, international organisation, government or company. It reflects seven years of international work across countries and topics and degrees from several countries, and it is the same whoever is asking. The total excludes VAT. Venue, hotel, hospitality and travel are never inside it: they are invoiced at cost, itemised. Once we have spoken for thirty minutes and know your actual departments, data and constraints, you get a fixed scope and a fixed price in writing, and that number is the one that binds.

What moves the day count: the level you are training to, the size of the organisation and of the departments involved, the depth of the assessment, the number of policy annexes, how many agents, skills and tools you want configured, and how many months of support. Two things do not move it: negotiating, and how urgent it is. We do not charge for speed and we do not discount the rate. The only reductions are the bundle lines you can see in the breakdown: training and policy bought together save a tenth of the days, and configuration bought with training saves a twentieth, because the people are already in the room.

The smallest engagement is three delivery days, EUR 2,400. For any engagement, and for individuals as well as groups, we can host the training in person and build it around what the participants need. The arrangements are agreed on the call and confirmed with the fixed price.

Would you like the number confirmed?

Thirty minutes. We check the scope, tell you if any part of it is unnecessary, and send the fixed scope and price the same week.
