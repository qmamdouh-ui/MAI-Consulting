01

Who you are contracting with.

M.A.I. Consulting is an independent consulting practice based in Geneva, Switzerland, serving clients internationally. It is a boutique practice with finite capacity and no bench, and the person who scopes an engagement is the person who delivers it.

Trading name

M.A.I. Consulting

Place of business

Geneva, Switzerland

Contact of record

q.mamdouh@mai4consulting.com

Governing law

Swiss law, with the courts of Geneva competent, unless the engagement letter records something else. For UN agencies and international organisations we will work within your standard contractual framework instead, including your own general conditions of contract.

Registration and tax numbers

To be confirmed Registration, VAT and bank details are provided in writing with the engagement letter and on any supplier form you send. Ask on the call and we will send them the same day.

02

Pricing, and what a price includes.

Every engagement is fixed price and fixed scope, agreed in writing before any work begins. The estimator shows an indicative range before we speak; the number that binds is the one in the engagement letter after a 30-minute call.

  • Rates differ by type of organisation. Taking the government and public rate as 1.0, an NGO or foundation pays 0.7×, an international organisation 0.9×, and a private-sector client 1.2×, for identical work.
  • Prices exclude VAT where VAT applies, and exclude travel.
  • A price includes discovery and scoping, delivery, the deliverables named in the engagement letter, and one round of revisions on each written deliverable.
  • We do not discount the rate and we do not charge a premium for urgency. Negotiating does not move the figure, and neither does a shorter deadline.
  • Courses for fewer than ten participants are priced per person. In-person courses include accommodation and hospitality arrangements, charged per participant per night and shown separately.
  • Support retainers are quoted monthly and billed monthly in advance.
03

Payment terms.

  • Delivery engagements: 40% on signature, 60% on delivery of the final deliverable.
  • Support retainers: billed monthly in advance.
  • Courses: 40% on registration, 60% before the course begins.
  • Portfolio programmes: as set out in the programme contract; a drawdown fund is invoiced when the block is committed, and any unused balance rolls over or is refunded as agreed at contract.
  • Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer in CHF unless another currency is agreed in writing.
  • Where your organisation's own payment terms are longer, say so before signature and we will price and schedule around them rather than discovering it at the first invoice.
04

Scope changes.

The scope is written down precisely so that both sides can tell when it has changed. Small adjustments inside an agreed scope are absorbed. A change of more than 20% in the work required is re-quoted in writing before it is done, rather than absorbed quietly or invoiced as a surprise.

Typical examples of a re-quote: departments added after the task inventory, a second language added after materials are drafted, a policy annex not in the original list, or an assessment extended to sites that were out of scope.

05

Cancellation and postponement.

Capacity is finite, so a cancelled engagement is a slot that cannot be refilled at short notice. These are the standing terms; anything different is recorded in the engagement letter.

More than 30 days before the first delivery date

Cancel at no charge. The 40% deposit is refunded in full, less any work already delivered and accepted at that point.

15 to 30 days before

The 40% deposit is retained. Nothing further is invoiced.

Fewer than 15 days before

The 40% deposit is retained and any work completed to that point is invoiced at the proportion delivered.

Postponement

One postponement is free if we have at least 15 days' notice and the new dates fall within six months. A second postponement, or less notice, is treated as a cancellation at the tier above.

Support retainers

Either side may end a retainer with 30 days' written notice. The month in progress is payable; nothing beyond it is.

Courses for individuals

Full refund up to 14 days before the start date. After that the registration fee is retained, but the place can be transferred to a colleague at no charge.

If we cancel

If we cannot deliver on the agreed dates for any reason within our control, everything paid is refunded in full and we help you find an alternative provider. We do not overcommit to win work, which is why this clause is rarely used.

06

Delivery, travel and languages.

  • Most delivery engagements run three to eight weeks. Support retainers run three to twelve months.
  • Sessions and materials are delivered in English, French or Arabic. Arabic material is written in Arabic from the first draft rather than translated.
  • Delivery is remote or on site. Travel outside Geneva is billed at cost, agreed in advance, and shown as a separate line rather than folded into the fee.
  • Attendance records, playbooks and reports are handed over in editable formats you can keep and reuse.
07

What you own at the end.

Everything produced for your organisation is yours: the assessment report and scores, the AI use policy and its annexes, the tool inventory, every department playbook, the attendance records, and any agents, skills or tools configured on your own platforms at your own account level.

We retain our own underlying method, instrument, question banks, templates and training material, which are used across engagements. Using them for you does not transfer them to you, and delivering for you does not restrict us from working with other organisations, including in the same sector.

We will not name you as a client, quote you, or use your material as an example without your written permission first.

08

Confidentiality and how your data is handled.

  • Everything we see in an engagement is confidential and is not disclosed to anyone outside the engagement. We will sign your own NDA or confidentiality annex.
  • Where the work involves personal, beneficiary or case data, the handling is agreed in writing before the work starts, including whether a data processing agreement is required and who the controller is.
  • We apply the same rules we write for you: personal and beneficiary data is not entered into AI tools without an agreement covering it, and identifiers are removed before any tool sees a document.
  • What this website itself collects, and where it goes, is set out in the privacy policy.
09

What this practice does not do.

Stated here as well as on the About page, because it belongs in the commercial terms as much as in the positioning.

  • No regulatory compliance services, no certification, no legal advice. Our documents use the vocabulary of recognised frameworks so they are legible to the people who ask about them. Whether that satisfies a particular legal obligation is a question for your own counsel, and we will say so rather than answer it.
  • No AI systems built. No model fine-tuning, no data pipelines, no custom software. We configure agents, skills and tools on platforms you already use, and stop there.
  • No decisions delegated to a tool. We will not configure anything that decides eligibility, safeguarding, hiring or payment without a documented human review.
  • No guarantee of a specific outcome. We commit to the deliverables named in the engagement letter, on the dates agreed. We do not guarantee a score, a productivity figure or a funding decision, and we will not put one in a proposal.
  • Liability is limited to the fees paid for the engagement, except where Swiss law does not permit that limit.
10

Supplier onboarding.

If your organisation needs us set up as a vendor before a contract can be signed, ask on the call and the pack follows the same day. It normally covers:

  • Legal entity name, place of business and contact of record
  • Registration and tax identifiers, and VAT status
  • Bank details for payment, on letterhead
  • Signed copies of your supplier code of conduct, anti-fraud, anti-harassment and safeguarding declarations
  • Confirmation of insurance where your framework requires it
  • Your own general conditions of contract, reviewed and signed
  • Completed UNGM, ERP or e-procurement registration where you use one

Sanctions and due-diligence screening is expected and we will complete whatever form your framework requires. If a requirement in your framework is one we cannot meet, we will tell you before you spend time on the paperwork rather than after.
