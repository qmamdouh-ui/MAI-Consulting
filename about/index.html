About the practice

A boutique built for one job.

M.A.I. Consulting makes organisations AI-fluent for the work they actually do. Four services, one framework, fixed prices. We do not build AI systems, and we do not sell anybody's software.

What the practice does

We work on the people who use AI, not the infrastructure that runs it.

Most AI failure in an organisation is not technical. Tools get bought, a generic session gets delivered, nothing changes in the work, and a policy written to eliminate risk pushes real use into private accounts. We address that specific failure and nothing else.

Diagnose before training anybody

A scored baseline across ten dimensions tells you where the gap actually is. Training a department that is already fluent, while another one is guessing, is the most common way this money gets wasted.

Write the permissions, not the prohibitions

A policy that only forbids makes necessary work non-compliant, so the work happens anyway and the organisation carries the risk without seeing it. Ours says what is allowed, with which data, by whom.

One page per team, built on their own tasks

Not a course, not a curriculum. The shortest set of instructions that changes what a department does on Monday — and nothing beyond it.

Leave behind a file, not a dependency

The score, the policy, the cheatsheets and the attendance record are yours. They are also the evidence a donor, an auditor or a regulator asks to see.

The method

One vocabulary across every deliverable.

Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence — the AI Fluency framework published by Anthropic with Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller. We use a public framework rather than a proprietary one so that you can audit the method instead of taking it on trust.

Delegation

What to hand to AI, what to keep human, and who decides.

Description

How to ask, with your organisation's context already in the request.

Discernment

How to check an output before anyone relies on it.

Diligence

What to log, what to disclose, and who owns the result.

The same four words are the axes of your score, the sections of every cheatsheet, the clause groups of your policy and the modules of every session. Nobody has to translate between the diagnosis, the training and the rules.

How engagements run

Being straight with you

What this practice is not.

It is not a large firm. There is no bench and no 24-hour turnaround. Capacity is finite and engagements are scheduled accordingly. If you need thirty trainers in four countries next month, we are the wrong call.

It does not build your AI systems. No model fine-tuning, no data pipelines, no custom deployment. Four services, and they are all about the people who use AI.

It sells no software. No licences resold, no partner commissions, no quota. That is what makes “stay on the free tier” something we can actually say to you.

It will point you elsewhere when that is right. Free introductory courses on this framework exist, published by the people who wrote it. Subsidised programmes exist for smaller nonprofits. Where one of those is the right first step, the discovery call will say so.

It is early. Founded in 2023, and the client list is short. What you can inspect before committing is the instrument, the framework, the sample deliverable and the prices — deliberately more than most consultancies put in front of you.
