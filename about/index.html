About

Who you would actually be working with.

M.A.I. Consulting is a boutique practice, not a firm with a pitch team. The person who scopes your engagement is the person who delivers it. Here is who that is, and what they have actually done.

Mouhamad Mamdouh Alqudsi · Founder

AI governance and technology policy, practised inside the sector it serves.

Currently Trade and Competitiveness Expert Consultant at the International Trade Centre, the joint agency of the United Nations and the World Trade Organization, working on its Iraq and Jordan portfolio. Co-developed two country-level strategies for Iraq on access to finance and entrepreneurship, and built an AI literacy course for entrepreneurs that translates AI capability and risk into applied guidance.

Earlier, at the Office of the UN Envoy on Technology in New York, delivered technical presentations on internet infrastructure, global connectivity and digital inclusion to the Under-Secretary-General and the Tech Envoy Office team, and prepared the analytical briefings behind them. Before that, coordinated multi-stakeholder dialogues for the UN's 75th anniversary commemoration.

On an EU-funded multi-city initiative with Grenoble Metropole, designed and ran a five-round consultation process across national institutions, civil society organisations and marginalised communities, converging on a single signed guide for newly arrived migrants. The method that produces an AI use policy your staff will actually follow is the same one.

The starting point was Damascus, coordinating relief during active conflict: a volunteer team that grew from three people to more than a hundred, and 300+ displaced families registered across five shelters. That is where the interest in what actually works — rather than what sounds right in a strategy document — comes from.

Built, not bought

The diagnostic on this site is our own.

Most boutiques improvise a maturity model in a slide. The instrument here is a ten-dimension, sixty-question assessment scoring 300 points normalised to 0–100, published free and open to anyone.

Dimensions 1–4 — fluency

Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence, adapting the AI Fluency framework developed by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller with Anthropic.

Dimensions 5–10 — governance and organisational readiness

Data Protection and Ethics, Governance and Compliance, Workforce Impact, Operations and Politics, Psychological Safety, and Anticipation and Future-Readiness.

Being straight with you

What this practice is not.

It is not a firm. There is no bench, no delivery team, no 24-hour turnaround. Capacity is finite and engagements are scheduled accordingly. If you need thirty trainers in four countries next month, we are the wrong call.

It does not build your AI systems. No model fine-tuning, no data pipelines, no custom deployment. Four services, and they are all about the people who use AI, not the infrastructure that runs it.

It will tell you to use the free option. Free introductory courses on this framework exist, published by the people who wrote it. Subsidised programmes exist for smaller nonprofits. Where one of those is the right first step, that is what the discovery call will say.

It is early. This practice was founded in 2023 and the client list is short. What you can inspect before committing is the instrument, the framework, the sample cheatsheet and the person. That is deliberately more than most consultancies let you see.

Thirty minutes is enough to know whether this fits.
