### AI Fluency (4D)

#### Delegation

_Knowing when & what to delegate to AI_

- Does your organization have a clear understanding of which tasks AI can and cannot do?
  - _1 = No awareness → 5 = Deep understanding_
- Have you identified specific, repetitive tasks in your workflow that AI could handle?
  - _Yes = 5 / No = 0_
- How many AI tools does your team currently use (even occasionally)?
  - _0=0pts, 1=1pt, … 5+=5pts_
- How confident is your team in deciding when to use AI vs. when to keep work human?
  - _1 = Not confident → 5 = Very confident_
- Has your organization mapped AI opportunities to its mission or strategic objectives?
  - Not at all (0)
  - Discussed (2)
  - Partially mapped (3)
  - Mostly mapped (4)
  - Fully mapped (5)

#### Description

_Communicating effectively with AI_

- When prompting AI, do you typically include context about your organization and role?
  - _1 = Never → 5 = Always_
- Do you know how to write a prompt that specifies a desired format, length, and tone?
  - _Yes = 5 / No = 0_
- How often do you iterate on your prompts (refine them based on AI responses)?
  - Never (0)
  - Rarely (1)
  - Sometimes (3)
  - Often (4)
  - Always (5)
- Can your team describe the desired output, process, and performance behaviour in a single prompt?
  - _1 = Cannot → 5 = Consistently_
- Do you provide AI with relevant organizational context (documents, briefs, examples) when the task warrants it?
  - _Yes = 5 / No = 0_
- How well does your team communicate constraints (word limits, audience, scope) to AI?
  - _1 = Poorly → 5 = Very well_
- Do you use structured conversation techniques (asking AI to interview you, reusable context docs, prompt templates)?
  - _Yes = 5 / No = 0_

#### Discernment

_Critically evaluating AI outputs_

- Does your team critically evaluate AI outputs before using them?
  - _1 = We trust everything → 5 = Always evaluate rigorously_
- Can you identify when an AI output contains outdated, inaccurate, or hallucinated information?
  - _1 = Rarely → 5 = Consistently_
- Do you verify key claims from AI with original or external sources?
  - _Yes = 5 / No = 0_
- Are individuals on your team able to spot biased, deficit-based, or stigmatizing language in AI outputs?
  - _Individual-level skill. 1 = Not yet → 5 = Reliably_
- How often does your team reflect on why AI produced a particular output (process discernment)?
  - Never (0)
  - Rarely (1)
  - Sometimes (3)
  - Often (4)
  - Always (5)
- Do you evaluate whether individual AI outputs align with your organization's values and mission?
  - _Per-output check. Yes = 5 / No = 0_
- When AI gives a poor response, can your team diagnose what went wrong with the prompt?
  - _1 = Never → 5 = Consistently_

#### Diligence

_Reviewing, disclosing, taking responsibility_

- Does your team take responsibility for reviewing AI-generated outputs before sharing them externally?
  - _1 = Rarely → 5 = Always_
- Do you disclose AI involvement when relevant (to funders, beneficiaries, partners, audiences)?
  - _Yes = 5 / No = 0_
- Does your team practice data hygiene in daily use (removing PII before sharing prompts/files with AI)?
  - _Daily practice. Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_
- How intentional is your organization about which AI tool to use for which task?
  - _1 = Whatever is available → 5 = Deliberate, documented choices_
- Is there a routine process to spot-check AI outputs already in production work (catching errors after the fact)?
  - _Routine quality check, distinct from incident handling. Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_

### Risk & Compliance

#### Data Protection & Ethics

_Privacy, lawful use, bias, ethical principles_

- Has your organization conducted a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for any AI system processing personal data?
  - Not aware DPIAs apply to AI (0)
  - Aware, never done (1)
  - Done informally (3)
  - DPIA(s) completed and documented (5)
- Do you have a documented list of data categories that must NOT be entered into external AI tools (PII, health, financial, beneficiary data)?
  - _Policy artifact. Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_
- How well do you understand the data-handling terms of the AI vendors you use (training opt-out, retention, region, sub-processors)?
  - _1 = Not at all → 5 = Thoroughly, for every vendor_
- Does your organization run protocols to assess AI outputs for harmful bias (gender, race, age, disability, language)?
  - _Organizational protocol (complements individual spotting in Discernment)._
  - Never (0)
  - Ad hoc when flagged (2)
  - Regularly for sensitive use cases (4)
  - Systematic bias-testing protocol (5)
- Is informed consent obtained when AI is used to process data of beneficiaries, clients, customers, or staff?
  - Not considered (0)
  - Inconsistently (2)
  - For most cases (4)
  - Always, with clear records (5)
- Has your organization adopted explicit ethical principles for AI use (fairness, transparency, human dignity, non-maleficence)?
  - No (0)
  - Informally discussed (2)
  - Written, not yet operationalized (3)
  - Adopted and integrated in workflows (5)
- Are sensitive AI use cases (those affecting vulnerable people, livelihoods, or rights) subject to an ethics review before deployment?
  - _Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_
- Which ethical AI standard(s) or framework(s) has your organization adopted or aligned with?
  - _Select all that apply. This question is informational and does not affect your score._
  - OECD AI Principles
  - UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI
  - EU Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI
  - IEEE Ethically Aligned Design
  - Asilomar AI Principles
  - Sector-specific code (e.g. WHO, OECD/G20 finance)
  - Internal/proprietary code only
  - None adopted yet

#### Governance & Compliance

  - _Consolidated policy question (covers acceptable use, prohibited tasks, oversight)._
  - No policy and no plans (0)
  - Discussing it (2)
  - Draft exists (3)
  - Policy approved and in force (5)
- Is there a named accountable owner for AI in your organization (executive sponsor, AI lead, ethics officer) with leadership backing?
  - No (0)
  - Informally (2)
  - Yes, role unclear (3)
  - Yes, formally appointed with mandate and leadership support (5)
- Do you maintain an inventory of AI systems in use, with purpose, data flows, and risk classification?
  - No inventory (0)
  - List of tools only (2)
  - Inventory with use cases (3)
  - Full inventory with risk tiers and data flows (5)
- How aligned is your organization with a recognized AI governance framework (maturity level)?
  - _Pair with the next question to record which framework(s)._
  - Not aware of frameworks (0)
  - Aware, not yet mapped (1)
  - Self-assessed against one (3)
  - Mapped against multiple with gap plan (4)
  - Certified or formally audited (5)
- For AI systems with significant impact on people, do you conduct (or plan) a Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment or equivalent?
  - _Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No / Unaware = 0_
- Are AI-related incidents (errors, near-misses, harms, complaints) formally logged and reviewed?
  - _Incident handling (distinct from routine spot-checks in Diligence)._
  - No process (0)
  - Informal awareness (2)
  - Logged ad hoc (3)
  - Documented incident process with reviews (5)
- Which AI governance / compliance standard(s) has your organization adopted or aligned with?
  - _Select all that apply. This question is informational and does not affect your score._
  - ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AI Management System)
  - NIST AI RMF 1.0
  - EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689)
  - GDPR / national data-protection law
  - ISO/IEC 23894 (AI risk management guidance)
  - ISO/IEC 27001 (information security)
  - Singapore Model AI Governance Framework
  - Sector regulator guidance (specify in "Other")
  - Other (specify below)
  - None adopted yet

### Human & Organizational

#### Workforce Impact

_Jobs, morale, distraction, role redesign_

- Has leadership assessed how AI is changing specific roles (tasks added, removed, redesigned)?
  - No (0)
  - Informal observation (2)
  - Reviewed for some teams (3)
  - Systematic role-impact analysis (5)
- Have you observed AI causing distraction, fragmentation, or "tool fatigue" among staff?
  - Major problem, not addressed (0)
  - Noticeable, no plan (1)
  - Noticeable, mitigation in progress (3)
  - Monitored, low impact (4)
  - Not an issue / actively managed (5)
- Is staff workload tracked to ensure AI reduces burden rather than adding hidden new work (review, prompting, verification)?
  - _Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_
- How would you describe staff morale regarding AI rollout in the last 6–12 months?
  - Declining noticeably (0)
  - Mixed, leaning negative (2)
  - Stable (3)
  - Mixed, leaning positive (4)
  - Engaged and positive (5)
- Has the organization committed (in writing or budgeted practice) to reskilling/redeployment rather than replacement where AI displaces tasks?
  - _Structural commitment (Psychological Safety captures the communication dimension)._
  - No, replacement is default (0)
  - Case-by-case (2)
  - Verbal commitment (3)
  - Documented commitment with budget (5)
- Are productivity gains from AI being measured against any losses (errors, rework, judgment failures, customer dissatisfaction)?
  - _Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_

_Decision rights, shadow AI, internal dynamics_

- Is decision-making authority for AI tool selection and use clearly defined across departments (IT, business owners, compliance)?
  - Unclear, contested (0)
  - Informally negotiated (2)
  - Defined but inconsistent (3)
  - Clear cross-functional governance (5)
- Is there evidence of "shadow AI", staff using AI tools the organization does not officially sanction or know about?
  - Widespread, no visibility (0)
  - Some, suspected (1)
  - Some, monitored (3)
  - Limited, with sanctioned alternatives (4)
  - Visible and managed (5)
- Has AI adoption created or amplified friction between departments (IT vs business, leadership vs frontline, central vs field)?
  - Major friction, unresolved (0)
  - Notable, being addressed (2)
  - Some, manageable (3)
  - Little, with collaboration forums (5)
- Are AI decisions (procurement, deployment, scope) AND the resulting shifts in influence/power transparent to affected staff?
  - _Merged: combines decision transparency and power-shift transparency._
  - Opaque (0)
  - Some visibility (2)
  - Mostly transparent (3)
  - Fully transparent with consultation (5)
- Is there a clear escalation path when AI use raises operational, ethical, or quality concerns?
  - _Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_

#### Psychological Safety

_Voice, fear, leadership behaviour_

- Do staff feel safe to express concerns, doubts, or fears about AI without negative career consequences?
  - _1 = Not at all → 5 = Very much so_
- How prevalent is fear of being replaced by AI among your team?
  - Widespread, unaddressed (0)
  - Noticeable, no plan (1)
  - Noticeable, being discussed (3)
  - Limited, with reassurance (4)
  - Minimal, trust is high (5)
- Has leadership communicated openly and concretely about how AI will (and will not) affect jobs?
  - _Communication dimension (Workforce Impact captures the structural commitment)._
  - No communication (0)
  - Vague statements (1)
  - General communication (3)
  - Clear, specific, repeated (5)
- Are there confidential channels (anonymous surveys, ombud, listening sessions) for staff to raise AI-related worries?
  - _Yes = 5 / Partial = 3 / No = 0_
- Do staff feel safe to flag AI errors or failures without blame?
  - _Cultural condition for incident reporting to work. 1 = Not at all → 5 = Strongly_
- Does leadership model ethical, balanced AI behaviour (acknowledges limits, admits uncertainty, names trade-offs, supports adoption visibly)?
  - _Combines leadership modelling and active sponsorship. 1 = Not at all → 5 = Consistently_
