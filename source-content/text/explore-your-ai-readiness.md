### Delegation

- Does your organization have a clear understanding of which tasks AI can and cannot do?
  - _1 = No awareness → 5 = Deep understanding_
- Have you identified specific, repetitive tasks in your workflow that AI could handle?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- How many AI tools does your team currently use (even occasionally)?
  - _0=0pts, 1=1pt, 2=2pts, 3=3pts, 4=4pts, 5+=5pts_
- Do you have a policy or shared understanding of which tasks should NOT be delegated to AI?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- How confident is your team in deciding when to use AI vs. when to keep work human?
  - _1 = Not confident → 5 = Very confident_
- Has your organization mapped AI opportunities to its specific mission or strategic objectives?
  - Not at all (0)
  - We have discussed it (2)
  - Partially mapped (3)
  - Mostly mapped (4)
  - Fully mapped (5)
- Does leadership in your organization actively support AI adoption decisions?
  - _1 = No support → 5 = Strong champions_

### Description

- When prompting AI, do you typically include context about your organization and role?
  - _1 = Never → 5 = Always_
- Do you know how to write a prompt that specifies a desired format, length, and tone?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- How often do you iterate on your prompts (refine them based on AI responses)?
  - Never (0)
  - Rarely (1)
  - Sometimes (3)
  - Often (4)
  - Always (5)
- Can your team describe the desired output (product), process, and performance behavior in a single prompt?
  - _1 = Cannot → 5 = Consistently do this_
- Do you upload relevant organizational documents (reports, proposals) to give AI better context?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- How well does your team communicate specific constraints (word limits, audience, privacy) to AI?
  - _1 = Poorly → 5 = Very well_
- Do you use structured conversation techniques (e.g., asking AI to interview you, building reusable context docs)?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_

### Discernment

- Does your team critically evaluate AI outputs before using them?
  - _1 = We trust everything → 5 = Always evaluate rigorously_
- Can you identify when an AI output contains outdated, inaccurate, or hallucinated information?
  - _1 = Rarely catch it → 5 = Consistently catch it_
- Do you verify key claims from AI with original or external sources?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- Are you able to spot when AI outputs use inappropriate, biased, or deficit-based language?
  - _1 = Not yet → 5 = Reliably_
- How often does your team reflect on why AI produced a particular output (process discernment)?
  - Never (0)
  - Rarely (1)
  - Sometimes (3)
  - Often (4)
  - Always (5)
- Do you evaluate whether AI outputs align with your organization's values and mission?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- When AI gives a poor response, can your team diagnose what went wrong with the prompt?
  - _1 = Never → 5 = Consistently_

### Diligence

- Does your team take responsibility for reviewing and vouching for all AI-generated outputs before sharing?
  - _1 = Rarely → 5 = Always_
- Do you disclose AI involvement when relevant (to funders, beneficiaries, partners)?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- Are you aware of the data privacy policies of the AI tools your organization uses?
  - Not at all (0)
  - Somewhat (2)
  - Mostly (3)
  - Fully aware (5)
- Does your team practice data hygiene (removing PII before sharing with AI)?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
- How intentional is your organization about which AI tools to use for which tasks (creation diligence)?
  - _1 = We use whatever is available → 5 = Very deliberate_
- Do you have (or plan to have) an organizational AI policy that covers ethics, transparency, and oversight?
  - No plans (0)
  - Discussing it (2)
  - Draft exists (3)
  - Policy in place (5)
- Is there a process to review AI outputs periodically and catch errors that go into your work?
  - _Yes = 5 pts / No = 0 pts_
