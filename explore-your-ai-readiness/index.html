What the AI Readiness Check measures

Twenty-eight questions across the four competencies of AI fluency. Each competency is scored separately, so the result tells you which one carries your gap rather than only how you did overall. The questions ask what your organisation actually does today, not what it intends to do.

AI Fluency (4D)

Delegation

Knowing which tasks to hand to AI, which to keep human, and who decides. Seven questions cover task mapping, the tools already in use, whether anything is off-limits, and whether leadership backs the decisions.

AI Fluency (4D)

Description

How the work is specified: whether context, audience, format and constraints reach the tool, whether source documents are supplied rather than described, and whether prompts are refined rather than abandoned.

AI Fluency (4D)

Discernment

How outputs are checked before anyone relies on them: figures traced to a source, facts checked against the original, and a review step that exists as a rule rather than an assumption.

AI Fluency (4D)

Diligence

What is logged, what is disclosed and who owns the result. Covers disclosure to funders and beneficiaries, ownership of AI-assisted work, and whether a policy exists at all.

Common questions

How long does the AI Readiness Check take?

About twelve minutes for twenty-eight questions. Your answers are saved in your own browser as you go, so you can stop and come back.

Is it really free, and what does it cost me?

It is free and no contact details are needed to see your result. The score, the breakdown by competency and the ranked list of what to fix first all appear on the page as you answer. Details are asked for only if you want the PDF.

What framework is it based on?

The four competencies of AI fluency: Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence. That framework was published by Anthropic with Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller, and it is public, so the instrument can be checked against it.

What is the difference between this and the Self-Assessment?

This check is twenty-eight questions across the four competencies and takes twelve minutes. The AI Readiness Self-Assessment is sixty questions across ten dimensions, adds governance, data protection, workforce impact and psychological safety, and takes about thirty minutes. Both are free and self-administered.

Do you keep my answers?

Your answers stay in your own browser and are never sent to us. Only if you download the PDF do we receive your details, your score and a copy of the report.
