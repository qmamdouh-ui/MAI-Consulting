FOUR SERVICES

Everything We Do Is AI

Buy one, or buy the package. Every engagement is fixed-price and fixed-scope, agreed before we start.

01 2–3 WEEKS

AI Readiness Assessment

“Where do we actually stand?”

A scored report across ten dimensions with a ranked 90-day priority list, mapped to ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and EU AI Act Article 4.
02 3–4 WEEKS

AI Use Policy

“What are our people allowed to do?”

A board-ready policy that starts with permissions, not prohibitions — permitted uses by role, red lines, a data classification table, disclosure rules and an approved tool list.
03 2–6 WEEKS

Team AI Training

“What do I do with AI in my job?”

One organisation-wide session, then a 90-minute lab per department built on that team's real tasks. Each lab ends with that team's one-page cheatsheet.
04 3–12 MONTHS

Fluency Support

“How do we stop this decaying?”

Monthly office hours, cheatsheet updates as the tools change, coaching for your internal champions, and a re-score at six months.

One framework holds the four together

Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence — the AI Fluency framework published by Anthropic with Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller. They are the axes of your score, the sections of every cheatsheet, the clause groups of your policy and the modules of every session. One vocabulary, so nobody has to translate between them.
