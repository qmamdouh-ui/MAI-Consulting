The twelve professionals are composite personas from M.A.I. training cohorts; names, organisations and figures are illustrative and describe realistic targets for each level rather than audited results. The levels and the four-part method follow the AI Fluency framework published by Anthropic with Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller (source), and match the capability ladder on our training page. Days to reach each level and the time saved on scoped tasks come from that page and its cited field studies.

What moves people up

The training between the levels.

Reaching level 3 is the shortest engagement we take, from either starting point. Above it, each step turns on a Claude capability and adds delivery days. Time saved is on the tasks in scope, not a whole job.
