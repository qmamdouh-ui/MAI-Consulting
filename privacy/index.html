Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, where it goes, how long it is kept and how to have it removed. Last updated September 2026. Data controller: M.A.I. Consulting, Geneva, Switzerland.

This policy describes what mai4consulting.com collects, why, where it goes and how long it is kept. It covers four things on this site that collect personal data: the contact form, the price estimator, the two free readiness assessments, and the site assistant. Each is described separately below, because they behave differently.

1. Who we are

M.A.I. Consulting is an AI training, policy and readiness practice based in Geneva, Switzerland, serving clients internationally. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website. For any privacy question, or to exercise a right below, write to q.mamdouh@mai4consulting.com.

2. What each part of the site collects

The contact form (/contact-us/) collects your name, email address and message, which are required, and your organisation, role, sector, organisation size, preferred contact method, phone number if you ask to be called, and how you heard about us, all of which are optional. It is sent to us by email so that we can reply.

The price estimator (/estimate/) does not collect anything while you use it. The selections you make stay in your browser and the range is calculated there. Only if you press Email me this scope do we receive anything: your name, email, organisation if you gave one, the selections you made, any notes you typed, and the range shown. A copy of the same email is sent to you.

The two readiness assessments (the free check and the self-assessment) work the same way. Your answers, your score and your recommendations are calculated in your browser and are saved in your browser's local storage so you can close the tab and resume; that copy stays on your device and is never sent to us. Nothing reaches us unless you press Download PDF Report. When you do, three things happen:

  • The PDF is generated and downloaded to your device.
  • A copy of that PDF is uploaded to a Google Drive folder we control, through a Google Apps Script endpoint, so that we hold the same report you do if you contact us about it.
  • An email is sent to us containing your name, organisation, role, sector, organisation size, email address, your overall score, your dimension scores, your triggered recommendations, and a link to that stored PDF.

If you do not want us to hold a copy, take the assessment and read the results on the page: the score, the breakdown and the recommendations are all shown there without giving us anything. You can also ask us to delete the stored report at any time and we will.

The site assistant answers from a fixed set of written answers held in the page. It calls no language model and sends nothing anywhere while you use it. What you type stays in your browser. Only if you choose to pass a question to the team at the end of a conversation are the transcript and the details you enter emailed to us, by the same route as the contact form.

Server logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records standard request information, including IP addresses, when pages are served. That processing is GitHub's, under its own privacy statement, and we neither receive nor look at those logs.

3. Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies. There is no advertising, no tracking pixel, no cross-site identifier and no profiling. You will not see a cookie banner here because there is nothing to consent to.

The site uses browser local storage in one place only: to save your assessment answers on your own device so a reload does not lose half an hour of work. It never leaves your browser and you can clear it with the Start over button on the assessment or by clearing site data.

Where we measure how the site is used, we use a cookieless analytics service that counts page views and a small number of interaction events, such as whether an estimate was generated or an assessment completed. It records no cookie, stores no IP address, sets no identifier, and cannot connect one visit to another or to you. No personal data, no form values and no assessment answers are ever sent to it.

4. Why we may process your data, and on what basis

  • To answer you. Where you send a message, an estimate or a report, we use your details to reply and to manage the conversation that follows. Basis: your consent, given by sending it, and our legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry.
  • To deliver services you ask for. Basis: performance of a contract.
  • To understand how the site is used, in aggregate and without identifying anyone. Basis: legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal obligations under Swiss law and, where it applies, the GDPR.

We do not sell, rent or trade your data, we do not add you to a marketing list because you used a free tool, and we do not send unsolicited mail.

5. Who else handles your data

EmailJS

Delivers the contact form, the estimator email and the assessment notification. It transmits the message and does not retain it beyond what delivery requires. United States.

Google (Drive and Apps Script)

Stores the PDF copy of a readiness report when you download one. Ireland and the United States.

GitHub Pages

Hosts and serves this website. United States.

Google Fonts

Serves the two typefaces used on this site. Your browser requests them directly, which discloses your IP address to Google for that request.

Cookieless analytics

Counts page views and interaction events. Receives no personal data and sets no identifier.

None of these is permitted to use your data for its own purposes. We use no other processor and share your data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

6. AI tools

We use AI tools, including Claude by Anthropic and Microsoft Copilot, in our own work. We do not put your personal data into them. Identifiers are removed before any document reaches a tool, and your data is never used to train any model, ours or anyone else's. The assistant on this site runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing to a language model at all.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries and correspondence

Up to 3 years from our last contact, then deleted.

Estimator submissions

Up to 3 years, as part of the enquiry they belong to.

Stored readiness reports

Up to 3 years, or immediately on request.

Client engagement records

For as long as Swiss accounting and tax law requires, currently 10 years for records with an accounting dimension.

Assessment answers in your browser

30 days, then cleared automatically. Under your control at all times.

Analytics

Aggregate counts only, with nothing that identifies a visitor.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) you may ask us to give you a copy of what we hold, correct it, delete it, hand it over in a portable format, restrict what we do with it, or object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect anything done lawfully before that.

Write to q.mamdouh@mai4consulting.com. We answer within 30 days and there is no charge. A deletion request covering an assessment report also removes the stored PDF.

9. Transfers outside Switzerland and the EEA

We operate from Geneva. The processors listed in section 5 are based in, or transfer data to, the United States. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with the Swiss addendum recognised by the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, or on an adequacy decision where one applies to that provider.

10. Security

Everything is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to enquiries and stored reports is limited to the person who answers them. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

11. Children

This site is for professionals and organisations. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has submitted data here, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when what the site does changes. The date below always reflects the current version, and material changes are noted on the site.

13. Complaints

If you think we have handled your data badly, tell us first and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner at edoeb.admin.ch, or to your own supervisory authority in the EEA or the United Kingdom.
