## assessment-svc (See the assessment service)
Page: services/assessment/

Two to three weeks. ## agents-tools (See agents, skills and tools)

Yes, within limits. There are three things, scoped separately. ## article4 (Read the Article 4 briefing)
Page: eu-ai-act-article-4/
Article 4 of the EU AI Act has applied since 2 February 2025. A July 2026 amendment (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) rewrote it: providers and deployers must take measures to support the development of AI literacy among staff, and contractors acting on their behalf, and they do not have to guarantee a specific level for any individual. The measures should be proportionate to role, context and risk. Department-specific training, a documented policy and an attendance record are what those measures look like on paper. Switzerland is outside the EU, so the Act reaches a Geneva organisation through its EU-facing activity. The site carries a briefing on what a 60-person organisation should do.

## levels (See the seven levels)
Page: services/training/#levels
Training is scoped by level. Level 1 is no working use; level 2 is occasional use with no method; level 3 is structured prompting and verification, which is the floor we train to and takes three delivery days from either starting point. Level 4 adds projects, custom instructions and skills, at five days. Level 5 is delegated work with agents and parallel workstreams, at six and a half. Level 6 is building agents and skills for colleagues, at eight. Level 7 is terminal-based agentic work with no coding background, at ten. Groups starting at level 3 or above get a day or two of credit. The full ladder, with what each level changes and what we plan against, is on the training page.
